## Project Idea

- Initial idea: **AIccessibility** — a visually beautiful, stateless WebMCP gallery where ChatGPT Voice and the visitor share a live page. The visitor can experience art through literal description, spatial exploration, poetry, story, or curatorial interpretation; converse about image regions; and navigate freely by intent or mood.
- Larger thesis: Accessibility is not a reduced alternative interface. An AIccessible site exposes semantic capabilities through WebMCP, while the user’s agent carries preferences and conversational state and adapts the page’s expression to the individual.

## Build Preferences

- Preferred pace: Fast, ambitious, and autonomous. Optimize for a complete standout experience rather than a deliberately diminished fallback.
- Likely support needs: Current WebMCP imperative API; ChatGPT built-in browser limitations; proof that ChatGPT Voice can discover and operate site tools in one continuous voice session; multimodal artwork perception; accessible interaction design; polished visual direction; ruthless one-week scope control.
- Notes for downstream commands:
  - Full ChatGPT Voice is a product requirement, not optional polish.
  - The site is stateless for user preferences; conversational/personalization state belongs to the agent. The site retains only ephemeral page state.
  - Interpretations may use both the image and curator-provided facts.
  - Navigation should be free and conversational so the gallery feels alive.
  - The product is for everyone, including blind and low-vision visitors; visual beauty and rigorous accessibility must coexist.
  - Separate observed image content, museum facts, interpretation, and creative invention.
  - Use public-domain or otherwise clearly licensed artworks and assets.
  - Participant’s explicit quality bar: “We’re here to make something amazing, not half of it.”
